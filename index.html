FAQ

How do I play? Drag blocks from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to squeeze it and score. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move, for combos. The game ends when no block fits.

What are Bonus Stars? Clear a line through a star block. Collect three to spin the Bonus Round wheel for a quick mini-game worth up to 10,000 points.

The leaderboard is empty / I can't see my score. The leaderboard uses Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app. Scores appear after you finish a game.

How do I see my friends? Open Leaderboard → Friends and allow Game Center friends. Invite friends with the Invite button (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or a link).

Can I turn off reminders? Yes — Settings → Reminders in the game, or iPhone Settings → Notifications → Squeezy Blocks.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Your best score is also kept in Game Center.
