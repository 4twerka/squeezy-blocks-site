How do I play?

Drag blocks from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to squeeze it and score. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move, for combos. The game ends when no block fits.

What are Bonus Stars?

Some blocks carry a gold star. Clear a line through one to collect it — three stars spin the Bonus Round wheel for a quick mini-game worth up to 10,000 points.

Why is the leaderboard empty?

Leaderboards use Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app — scores appear after you finish a game.

How do I play with friends?

Open Leaderboard → Friends and allow Game Center friends. Use Invite to send a link over WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram.

Can I change how fast blocks move?

Yes — Settings → Drag Speed, from 1.0× (moves with your finger) to 3.5×.

Can I turn off reminders?

Yes — Settings → Reminders in the game, or iPhone Settings → Notifications → Squeezy Blocks.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically on your device, and your best score is kept in Game Center.
