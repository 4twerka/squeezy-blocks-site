How do I play?

Drag blocks from the tray onto the 8×8 board. Fill a whole row or column to squeeze it and score. Clear several lines at once, or keep clearing move after move, for combos. The game ends when no block fits.

What are Bonus Stars?

Some blocks carry a gold star. Clear a line through one to collect it — three stars spin the Bonus Round wheel for a quick mini-game worth up to 10,000 points.

Why is the leaderboard empty?

Leaderboards use Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iPhone Settings app — scores appear after you finish a game.

How do I play with friends?

Open Leaderboard → Friends and allow Game Center friends. Use Invite to send a link over WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram.

What is Jelly Jigsaw?

A second game mode on the main menu: 150 shape puzzles. Drag all the pieces into the glowing target shape so they fill it exactly. Pieces can't be rotated; from level 100 some pieces are decoys that don't belong. Your progress is saved and you can replay any solved level from the level map.

What are seasonal events?

Around Halloween, New Year, Valentine's Day, Easter and your own birthday, the game changes its look and brings two limited skins: one is a free gift, the other unlocks after 3 games during the event. Events start and end by themselves — no update needed — and skins you unlock stay yours forever.

How do I win medals?

Reach the top 3 of the daily, weekly or all-time Game Center leaderboard. Each time you climb back to #1 you earn another one. Your medals show in Profile, and other players can see them on your player card.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. The game speaks English, Español, Українська, Polski, Русский, Italiano and 日本語, and follows your iPhone's language by default.

Can I turn off the sound?

Yes — Settings → Sound, or the speaker button during a game. The game also stays quiet when your iPhone's silent switch is on.

Can I change how fast blocks move?

Yes — Settings → Drag Speed, from 1.0× (moves with your finger) to 3.5×.

Can I turn off reminders?

Yes — Settings → Reminders in the game, or iPhone Settings → Notifications → Squeezy Blocks.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically on your device, and your best score is kept in Game Center.
