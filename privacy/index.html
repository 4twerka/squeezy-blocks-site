Privacy Policy

Squeezy Blocks · Last updated October 16, 2026

✓

Short version: we collect no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking — and we don't run any servers.

📱

Your progress stays on your iPhone

Best score, saved game, settings (skin, drag speed, sound, reminders) and your daily streak are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

🏆

Game Center (optional)

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your scores go to Apple to show leaderboards, and your Game Center name and photo are visible to other players. Your friends list is only used if you allow it when the game asks. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy — we never receive your Apple ID, email or contacts.

🔔

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, the game schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us, and you can turn them off any time.

💬

Inviting friends

Invite opens WhatsApp, Telegram or Instagram, or copies a link with a short invite message. What you send is between you and that app — we receive nothing.

🚫

No ads, analytics or tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no tracking, and never uses your data across other companies' apps or websites.

🧒

Children

Squeezy Blocks is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes & contact
