Privacy Policy

Squeezy Blocks · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Short version: Squeezy Blocks does not collect, store or share any personal data on our servers. We don't have servers.

What stays on your device

Your game progress — best score, saved game, settings (skin, drag speed, sound, reminders) and daily streak — is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Game Center

If you sign in to Apple Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple to show leaderboards, and your Game Center name and photo are shown to other players. Seeing your Game Center friends only happens if you allow it when the game asks. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never receive your Apple ID, email or contacts.

Notifications

If you turn reminders on, the game schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Inviting friends

When you use Invite, the game opens WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram or copies a link with a short invite message. What you send is between you and that app; we receive nothing.

Tracking, ads and analytics

The game contains no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and does not use your data across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: vlapodev@gmail.com.
